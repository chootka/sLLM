# Blank vs organism

Steps 7 and 8 of the recording protocol, run as a pair.

## Plain English

The same dish twice: one day with no organism and fresh agar, then the organism
back on the same blobs and the same electrodes. If the rhythm is the organism,
it is absent on the first day and present on the second.

## Result

Hours with p <= 0.01 in the 60-200 s band:

| window | ch0 | ch1 | ch2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| blank, 14 h | 0/14 | 0/14 | 0/14 |
| organism, 21 h | 19/21, +1.64 to +3.22 dex | 9/21 | 0/21 |
| after removal, 14 h | 0/14 | 0/14 | 0/14 |

Decision rule row 2: the line does not reproduce without the organism.

## What it does not establish

Whether the line is the organism's own rhythm or electrochemistry at a
colonised electrode. Removal takes both away together. `blue-light-response`
is the test that separates them.
