# Noise floor

Step 5 of the recording protocol.

## Plain English

An empty dish, wired exactly as a real run, recorded for long enough to see
what the chamber alone produces. Anything the organism does has to be bigger
than this.

## Why the first hours are discarded

Fresh agar settles for 6-12 h. Readings inside that window are the gel, not the
chamber. Step 7 was lengthened from 6 h to 24 h for the same reason.

## Status

Never run as its own step. The pre-bridge window of run 6 has served as the
blank. `blank-vs-organism` is the stronger version and supersedes it: same dish
and same agar blobs, so the comparison holds more than a different day would.
