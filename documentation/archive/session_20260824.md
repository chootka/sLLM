### Methods as built, as of 2026-08-24

Instrumentation. Four Ag/AgCl electrodes, tips chloridized electrolytically over
5 mm. One MCP604 CMOS quad op-amp as unity-gain follower per electrode, VDD 3V3,
inputs biased to 1.65 V through 10 MΩ. 10 kΩ + 100 nF at each ADC pin, low-pass
159 Hz. ADS1115 16-bit ADC at 8 SPS, which notches 50 and 60 Hz. Recorded
differentially as E - REF, three channels against one shared reference. Raspberry
Pi 5. Electrodes enter on shielded Cat6 through a sealed grommet with a drip
loop; shield grounded at the board only, never inside the chamber.

Arena. 150 mm Petri dish in a plain plastic chamber, 25 C, 95-97% RH. Four
islands of non-nutrient 2% agar on the bare dish floor: reference 20 mm dia x
4 mm, three recording islands 15 mm dia x 4 mm, 10 mm of bare floor edge to
edge, 27.5 mm centre to centre. All four tips laid flat on the dish floor at one
depth and agar dropped over them. Bare oat flake on each recording island, none
on the reference. Plasmodium inoculated on the reference island. LEDs off, panel
held dark, timelapse at 300 s.

Departures from Adamatzky and Jones 2011: 150 mm arena rather than 90 mm; three
recording electrodes rather than up to eight; discrete islands separated by
10 mm of bare floor rather than blobs at unstated spacing.

### Instrument characterization — citable

*Measured 2026-08-24 01:34:49-02:01 CEST.*

Measured with electrodes removed and the four buffer inputs tied into one node,
27 min at 8 SPS:

| | drift over 27 min | HF noise, shorted | HF noise, in solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| ch0 | +0.025 mV | 0.037 mV | 0.026 mV |
| ch1 | +0.004 mV | 0.020 mV | 0.026 mV |
| ch2 | +0.031 mV | 0.182 mV | 0.060 mV |

HF noise is the standard deviation of the sample-to-sample difference divided by
sqrt(2), which is immune to slow drift. In-solution noise at or below the
shorted floor demonstrates the chamber picks up no significant interference; no
electrical shielding was fitted. No day/night pattern over 26 h (ch2
0.040-0.096 mV).

Fixed inter-channel offsets 0.2-0.9 mV, stable to 0.053 mV over the settled
hours. Buffers are unity gain, so amplitudes are comparable across channels.

Against the 2-5 mV amplitudes reported by Adamatzky and Jones, signal to noise
is 50-200x.

### Negative results worth reporting

1. A 20 min recording taken 50 min after immersion showed 2.7 mV of apparent
   drift on ch2. A 26 h record showed all three channels converging to within
   0.35 mV. The apparent drift was the settling transient. Drift must not be
   attributed from a window shorter than the settling time.
2. An earlier ~50 s rhythm was an artifact of common-mode subtraction against
   the shared reference, not biological. Signals appearing identically on all
   three channels indicate reference movement.
3. The continuous agar bed used previously shunts the source. Computed divider
   30:1 to 300:1; a 5 mV tube potential arrives as 17-170 µV.
